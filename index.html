Our focus

Believers transformed and empowered to make a positive impact in their communities and the world.

Faith lived out in practical ways.

We seek to see believers transformed and empowered to make a positive impact in their communities and the world through biblical teaching, discipleship, spiritual development, practical Christian living, seminars, workshops, talk shows, counselling, outreaches, and retreats.

In addition to spiritual development, the Foundation supports individuals and communities through skill acquisition, school-fee assistance, economic empowerment, and medical support — recognizing that meaningful Christian growth involves both spiritual maturity and practical care for human needs.

Vision To see believers holistically transformed and empowered to make a positive impact in their communities and the world.
Mission To promote holistic spiritual, economic, and social growth among believers through biblical teaching, discipleship, spiritual development, and practical Christian living.
